How to read this sector (insurance)

A life insurer's value is the embedded value of policies already sold plus the value of new business (VNB) it keeps writing. Read VNB growth and VNB margin (profitability of new sales), persistency (do customers keep paying?) and solvency (can it fund growth?). Accounting P&L understates a growing insurer because new-business strain front-loads costs, so P/EV and operating RoEV matter more than P/E or P/B. For general insurers, a combined ratio above 100% means underwriting loses money and profits rely on investment income.
