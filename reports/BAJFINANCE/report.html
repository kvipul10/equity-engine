How to read this sector (nbfc)

An NBFC is a bank without cheap deposits: its edge must come from spread and underwriting, and its risk from funding. Read the spread (yield on AUM minus cost of borrowing) and credit cost together; then check the funding mix and the ALM gap, because NBFCs fail on liquidity before they fail on solvency (IL&FS, DHFL). AUM growing much faster than equity, a rising stage-2 book or a high commercial-paper share are the early warnings.
