How to read this sector (fmcg)

FMCG value comes from volume growth at stable gross margins, funded by suppliers and distributors (negative working capital). Split revenue growth into volume and price/mix: price-led growth without volume is borrowed from the future. Gross margin should hold through commodity swings (pass-through); A&P spend is the reinvestment in the brand, so a margin gain made by cutting A&P is low quality. Returns are very high because capital needs are tiny: watch for acquisitions diluting ROIC.
