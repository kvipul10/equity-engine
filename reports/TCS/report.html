How to read this sector (it services)

IT services sell people-hours: revenue = headcount x utilisation x price, and the cost base is mostly salaries. Read constant-currency growth (strips out the rupee), deal wins (TCV / book-to-bill) as the lead indicator, and utilisation and attrition as the margin levers. Employee cost % is the margin story; DSO including unbilled revenue catches revenue recognised ahead of billing. Treasury income is large, so check other income / PBT.
