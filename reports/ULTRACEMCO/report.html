How to read this sector (cement)

Cement is regional and capacity-driven: prices follow utilisation in each region, and margins follow power, fuel and freight costs. Read EBITDA per tonne (the industry's unit margin), capacity utilisation and capacity growth; acquisitions of stressed capacity are common, so check ROCE and EV per tonne of capacity after deals. Through-cycle ROCE is the honest return measure, and leverage at the trough tests the balance sheet.
