How to read this sector (utilities)

Regulated generators earn a return on regulated equity set by CERC (base RoE 15.5% on 30% normative equity for 2024-29), so the questions are: is reported RoE close to the allowed RoE, is capacity (and regulated equity) growing, and are discoms paying? Leverage is judged against the 70:30 normative debt-equity, not against unregulated peers. Receivable days from discoms and PLF (plant load factor) drive cash flow and incentives; renewables growth changes the risk profile toward merchant/tariff-bid returns.
