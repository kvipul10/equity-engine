How to read this sector (capital goods defence)

Order-book businesses: revenue today is yesterday's order book executed. Read order book / revenue (years of visibility), book-to-bill (orders won vs revenue booked) and the execution rate. Customer advances fund working capital (defence PSUs run negative working capital); falling advances or rising receivables signal stretched customers. Margins move with mix and indigenisation; one large order can swing a year, so read multi-year averages.
