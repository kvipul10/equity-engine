How to read this sector (real estate)

Developers recognise revenue only when projects complete, so the P&L lags the business by 2-4 years. Lead with cash: pre-sales (bookings) show demand, collections show whether customers pay, and operating cash flow shows what is left after construction and land spend. Inventory months (unsold stock / monthly sales) shows how long the land bank takes to monetise. Margins on recognised revenue reflect projects sold years ago; leverage should be judged against pre-sales and collections.
