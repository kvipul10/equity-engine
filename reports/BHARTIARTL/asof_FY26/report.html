How to read this sector (telecom)

Telecom is a fixed-cost network business: revenue = subscribers x ARPU, and margins expand as ARPU rises on a largely fixed base. Capex (network, 5G) and spectrum are the big capital calls, and lease liabilities (towers) sit inside debt after Ind AS 116, so judge leverage on net debt including leases and deferred spectrum dues. In a three-player market, tariff hikes drive ARPU; subscriber growth is now mostly mix (upgrades to 4G/5G, postpaid).
