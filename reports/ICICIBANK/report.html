How to read this sector (bank)

Judge a bank after credit costs, not before: RoA = NII/A + fees/A - opex/A - provisions/A - tax/A, and RoE = RoA x leverage. NIM and fees show earning power; credit cost, GNPA/NNPA and PCR show what the loan book really earns. CRAR and CASA decide how long growth can last: fast loan growth with falling capital is the classic Indian blow-up pattern. Read asset quality and capital before profit growth.
