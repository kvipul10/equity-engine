How to read this sector (holdco)

A holding company / incubator is a portfolio: consolidated ratios average very different businesses (trading, mining, airports, roads, new energy) at different stages. Read the segment mix, the standalone company (where the cash, debt and guarantees sit) versus consolidated, and value it as a sum of the parts with a holding-company discount. Rapid incubation means negative FCF and rising debt by design: judge whether the incubated businesses reach returns above their cost of capital, and watch guarantees and related-party exposures.
