How to read this sector (consumer manufacturing)

Branded consumer manufacturers (paints, adhesives, durables) combine FMCG-style brands and distribution with heavier plants and positive working capital. Read volume growth versus price/mix, gross margin through raw-material cycles (crude derivatives for paints), A&P and dealer reach as the moat, and capex: new capacity must earn back its cost, so watch fixed-asset turnover and ROIC after each expansion. Competition shows up first in volume share and A&P intensity.
