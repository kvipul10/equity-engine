How to read this sector (pharma)

Indian pharma has two different engines: the branded domestic franchise (steady, high-margin, marketing-led) and exports, especially US generics (price erosion, FDA compliance, lumpy launches) or specialty brands (R&D-heavy, high-margin). Read the US vs India mix, R&D intensity and the ANDA pipeline as the future, and FDA actions as the key risk. Exceptional items (settlements, impairments) are frequent: check exceptional frequency before trusting PAT growth.
