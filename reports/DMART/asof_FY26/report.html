How to read this sector (retail)

Retail growth = more stores x more sales per store. Separate store additions from same-store (like-for-like) growth: new stores can hide a slowing mature estate. Revenue per square foot measures productivity. After Ind AS 116 lease rentals sit below EBITDA, so compare margins and leverage on a lease-adjusted (pre-Ind AS 116) basis, especially versus owners of stores (DMart owns many). Inventory turns and supplier credit drive working capital; thin margins make cost discipline everything.
